# Fallen Lineage Beginner's Starter Guide

Welcome to the definitive progression handbook for Fallen Lineage on Roblox. Because Fallen Lineage incorporates turn-based mechanics with roguelite permadeath or run-based penalties, knowing how to spend your early resources is essential for a smooth playthrough.

## Your First Hour: Step-by-Step Checklist

1. **Claim Active Codes**: Before starting your first run, check our [Codes](/codes) page and redeem all current active codes for starting currency and rerolls.
2. **Master the Turn Order Gauge**: Fallen Lineage uses an initiative-based timeline. Actions with shorter recovery times allow you to act again before slow enemy attacks land. Never commit to high-cost heavy attacks if an enemy is scheduled to act next.
3. **Run Low-Risk Tutorial Encounters**: Focus on practicing resource consumption (Stamina/Mana) on low-tier dungeon rooms rather than rushing deep into the crypts.
4. **Bank Your Currency**: Always return to safe camp zones or hub shrines to deposit gold or essence before entering high-risk boss chambers.

## Currencies and Progression Economy

Understanding the resource cycle will prevent you from being soft-locked:

- **Gold / Coins**: The primary operational currency used to purchase basic consumables, health potions, and early gear upgrades at hub merchants.
- **Gems / Soul Shards**: Rare currency awarded from dungeon boss clears, daily quests, and redemption codes. Used for permanent talent unlocks and class rerolls.
- **Mastery Tokens**: Gained strictly by playing specific character classes in high-difficulty encounters; required to unlock tier-3 passive nodes.

## Common Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Neglecting Defense & Resistances**: Stacking pure offensive stats will leave you vulnerable to one-shot attacks in tier-2 dungeons. Always invest in baseline armor and evasion.
2. **Hoarding Low-Tier Consumables**: Consumables are meant to sustain your early runs. Using a potion early to save a revive will preserve your multiplier.
3. **Ignoring Class Strengths**: Trying to play a squishy mage as a brawler or playing a frontline tank passively will drag out battles and expose you to attrition damage. Review our [Classes Guide](/classes) to understand proper skill loops.
4. **Skipping the Official Community**: Game formulas and stealth balance adjustments happen regularly on Roblox. Consult the [Official Trello](/trello) to stay updated on mechanics.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What happens when I die during a run?
Fallen Lineage uses roguelite mechanics. While you may lose unbanked temporary dungeon loot from that specific run, your account-wide talents, unlocked class masteries, and premium currencies persist permanently.

### Can I change my class later?
Yes. You can unlock additional classes or reroll your primary archetype at the central Shrine using Gems or progression tokens earned in-game.

### Is Fallen Lineage solo friendly?
Absolutely. All standard content scales according to party size. Solo adventurers can clear full dungeons with proper turn sequencing and defensive preparation.
