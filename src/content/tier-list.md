## How Tiers Are Positioned

- **S-Tier**: Exceptional clear speed, independent survivability, minimal reliance on rare external drops.
- **A-Tier**: Consistent, dependable archetypes that require slight team setup or positioning.
- **B-Tier**: Specialized classes requiring higher stat investment to match top performers.
