# Fallen Lineage Official Trello Directory

The official Trello board is the primary documentation hub maintained by the Fallen Lineage development team.

## Verified Official Board Link

- **Direct Trello Link**: [https://trello.com/c/TJdNiq1k](https://trello.com/c/TJdNiq1k)
- **Access Rule**: The board is read-only for public community members; no Trello account is needed to inspect cards.

## Column Breakdown

1. **Information & Rules**: Guidelines, official Roblox group links, and combat terminology.
2. **Upcoming Roadmap**: In-progress biomes, class reworks, and balance candidates.
3. **Class Directory**: Character formulas, unlock costs, and skill tree paths.
4. **Items & Drops**: Loot tables, rarity brackets, and potion effects.
5. **Boss Directory**: Attack patterns, phase triggers, and crowd control tolerances.
6. **Known Issues**: Bug tracker and scheduled maintenance hotfixes.
