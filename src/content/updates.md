# Fallen Lineage Patch Notes & Update History

Track all recent updates, balance adjustments, hotfixes, and content expansions for Fallen Lineage in reverse chronological order.

---

## Update v1.0.4 – November 2026
*Released: November 2026*

### Major Highlights & Balance Changes
- **Class Balancing**: Adjusted cooldowns and mana consumption across elemental archetypes. Review the updated rankings on our [Class Tier List](/tier-list).
- **Dungeon Progression**: Enhanced floor boss drop rates and added safety checkpoints for solo runs. See tips in our [Beginner Guide](/guides/beginner).
- **System Stability**: Resolved turn-gauge synchronization glitches during co-op dungeon encounters.
- **New Promo Codes**: Added milestone reward codes for the community celebration. Check working entries on the [Roblox Codes](/codes) page.

## Update v1.0.3 – November 2026
*Released: Early November 2026*

### Changes & Optimizations
- **Class Skill Trees**: Expanded tier-2 passive branches for melee vanguard classes. Check detailed role guides on the [Classes](/classes) page.
- **Trello Board Sync**: Official development team reorganized roadmap cards and item tables. Check the breakdown on our [Trello Directory](/trello).
